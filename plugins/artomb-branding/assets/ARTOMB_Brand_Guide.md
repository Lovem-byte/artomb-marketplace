# ARTOMB Recruitment — Brand & Template Guide

Source of truth: extracted from `ARTOMB_Recruitment_PowerPoint_Template.pptx` (the official company template, uploaded by Love Muili on 2026-09-14). A full copy of that template is saved alongside this file at `plugins/artomb-branding/assets/ARTOMB_PowerPoint_Template.pptx`.

## 1. Colors

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Purple (primary / logo) | `#1F0248` | Dark slide/page backgrounds, primary logo color, section headers on light backgrounds |
| Secondary Purple | `#3B1C68` | Decorative accents, secondary shapes, numbered circle fills |
| Accent Gold | `#D5A546` | Subtitles, bullet accents, callout numbers, taglines, links/highlights — the one "pop" color, use sparingly |
| Ink / Body Text | `#242330` | Default body copy on light backgrounds |
| Muted Gray-Purple | `#6B6874` | Footers, captions, page numbers, secondary/small print |
| Light Tint | `#F5F2F8` | Card backgrounds, table shading, subtle section dividers on light pages |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text/logo on dark backgrounds, light-page background |

Do not introduce other colors. If a chart needs more than the palette above, use tints/shades of Deep Purple, Secondary Purple, and Accent Gold rather than unrelated hues.

## 2. Fonts

- **Headings (titles, section headers, numbers, "Thank You," etc.):** Georgia, Bold. Italic Georgia is used for pull-quotes/testimonials and the tagline.
- **Body copy (paragraphs, bullets, table text, captions):** Arial, Regular. Use Arial Bold for emphasis within body text — never bold Georgia for body copy or use Arial for headings.
- Do not substitute other fonts (no Calibri, no Times New Roman, no system defaults) in any ARTOMB deliverable. If Georgia or Arial is unavailable in the target tool, use the closest available serif (for Georgia) or sans-serif (for Arial) and note the substitution to Love.

### Typographic scale (from the source deck, in points — adapt proportionally for docx/xlsx/pdf)

| Element | Font | Size | Weight/Style | Color |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cover/title slide title | Georgia | 44pt | Bold | White (on Deep Purple bg) |
| Section divider number ("01") | Georgia | 110pt | Bold | Accent Gold |
| Section divider title | Georgia | 40pt | Bold | White |
| Content slide / page title | Georgia | 30pt | Bold | Deep Purple `#1F0248` |
| Body bullets / paragraph text | Arial | 15–16pt | Regular | Ink `#242330` (or Accent Gold for short emphasis bullets on some layouts) |
| Testimonial / pull-quote | Georgia | 27pt | Bold Italic | White (on Deep Purple bg) |
| Closing "Thank You" | Georgia | 40pt | Bold | White |
| Footer text / page number | Arial | 9pt | Regular | Muted Gray-Purple `#6B6874` |

For Word/PDF documents, a practical equivalent scale is: Title 26–28pt Georgia Bold, H1 18–20pt Georgia Bold, H2 14pt Georgia Bold, body 11pt Arial, footer/caption 9pt Arial.

## 3. Logo

Three approved logo assets (in this same `assets/` folder):

1. **Icon mark, deep purple** (`logo-icon-purple.png`) — the "AR" monogram inside a bracket-and-dots frame with a small triangle, color `#1F0248`, transparent background. Use on white/light backgrounds, typically small and top-left, paired with a typed page/section title.
2. **Icon mark, white/reversed** (`logo-icon-white.png`) — same mark in white, transparent background. Use small on Deep Purple (or other dark) backgrounds, e.g. footer corner of section dividers.
3. **Full lockup, white/reversed** (`logo-lockup-white.png`) — the icon mark stacked above the "ARTOMB RECRUITMENT" wordmark (Georgia Bold caps), white, transparent background. Use large and centered (or top-left) on Deep Purple backgrounds for cover slides and closing/"Thank You" slides. There is no full-color (purple) version of the full lockup in the source template — on light backgrounds, use the purple icon mark alone plus a typed "ARTOMB RECRUITMENT" wordmark in Georgia Bold if the full lockup is needed (e.g. a letterhead), matching the same proportions as the white lockup.

**Rules:** Never recolor, rotate, skew, or stretch the mark. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the dots in the frame. Always use the purple version on light backgrounds and the white/reversed version on dark or photographic backgrounds — never purple-on-dark or white-on-light.

## 4. Tagline & contact block

- Tagline: *"Building Careers. Empowering Businesses."* — always italic, small, placed near a logo or in a footer/closing area. Gold on dark backgrounds used for a cover slide, white italic on a closing slide; use Deep Purple or Accent Gold italic on light backgrounds.
- Standard contact line (closing slides, letterheads, one-pagers): `hello@artombrecruitment.com  •  www.artombrecruitment.com  •  [phone]` — separated by bullet points (`•`), Arial, Accent Gold on dark backgrounds.
- Footer wordmark on every page/slide: "ARTOMB RECRUITMENT" in small caps, Arial 9pt, Muted Gray-Purple `#6B6874`, bottom-left, paired with a page number bottom-right in the same style.

## 5. Layout patterns (from the 11-layout template)

Two background modes only — do not invent new ones:

- **Dark mode:** Deep Purple `#1F0248` full background, white headline text, Accent Gold for numbers/subtitles/taglines, a large soft Secondary-Purple circle bleeding off the top-right or bottom-right corner as the only decorative element. Used for: cover/title page, section dividers, testimonials/pull-quotes, closing page.
- **Light mode:** White background, Deep Purple headings, Ink-colored body text, Accent Gold for bullet markers/callout numbers/stat figures, Light Tint `#F5F2F8` for card or box backgrounds. Used for: agenda, standard content/bullet pages, two-column comparisons, content-plus-image pages, stats ("By the Numbers") pages, numbered process pages.

Recurring structural rules: a small purple icon mark sits top-left beside every light-mode page's title; the "ARTOMB RECRUITMENT" + page-number footer appears on every page; section/step numbers are always Accent Gold or set inside a solid Deep-Purple or Secondary-Purple filled circle with a white numeral.

## 6. Applying this to non-PowerPoint deliverables

- **Word documents / PDFs:** White page, Deep Purple Georgia Bold headings, Arial 11pt body, the purple icon mark plus "ARTOMB RECRUITMENT" wordmark as a simple letterhead top-left (or centered on a cover page), Muted Gray-Purple footer with the tagline or contact line, Accent Gold used sparingly for a rule line under the title, pull quotes, or callout boxes/tables (Light Tint fill with Deep Purple text).
- **Excel workbooks:** Header row filled Deep Purple with white Arial Bold text; Accent Gold for a single highlight column, key totals, or conditional-formatting accents; Light Tint for alternating row shading; Georgia Bold for any titles/section labels above tables; Arial for all cell data; company name/tagline in a small header/footer.
- **Forms (web or PDF):** White or Light Tint background, Deep Purple Georgia Bold section headings, Arial labels/body, Accent Gold for required-field markers or the submit button, purple icon mark in the header, footer with the ARTOMB contact line.
- Always include the ARTOMB wordmark or icon mark somewhere on page 1 (header, cover, or letterhead) and the standard footer treatment on every subsequent page, unless Love asks for a version without branding (e.g. an internal working draft).
